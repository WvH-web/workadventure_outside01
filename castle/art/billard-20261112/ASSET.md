# Castle Billiards Furniture

Generated with the built-in image tool for this castle, then transparently
trimmed and reduced to 256 pixels wide with the existing approved room workflow.
The full generated source and exact hashes are retained. This is a furnishing
sprite, not a rendered gameplay state. Actual ball state belongs to the separate
school billiards service.

Final generation prompt:

```text
Create one polished transparent PNG pixel-art furniture asset for a magical boarding-school castle RPG. Use case: stylized-concept, game sprite, not a scene or UI. The asset is a small welcoming billiards nook: one finely carved walnut pool table with deep emerald green felt, six clearly defined pockets, tiny numbered billiard balls and a wooden cue resting neatly on its rail, plus exactly two matching upholstered stools, one detached at the left end and one detached at the right end. Warm brass corner details, a little loving craftsmanship, restrained burgundy accents. The long table axis must be strictly horizontal, camera facing straight north with the familiar slightly elevated 3/4 top-down perspective of a classic 2D tile RPG, NOT a diagonal isometric rotation. Show enough top surface for it to be unmistakably a pool table. Two stools face inward toward the table, placed slightly lower than its center line, their sitting surfaces exposed for 32-pixel player avatars. Composition: very wide 2:1 silhouette, centered table body occupies about 60 percent of the total group width, stools each about 12 percent of the group width with a clear walkable gap from the table. Small RPG furniture scale: final group will occupy 256 by 128 game pixels, table about 160 by 80 pixels and stools about 32 pixels wide. Maintain distinct forms at that small size; don't paint miniature photographic detail. Rich hand-crafted pixel-art shading, crisp readable edges, warm amber indoor light, consistent soft grounded shadows that preserve alpha. Entire subject visible with margins, isolated on genuine transparency. No room, wall, floor, rug, lettering, signage, UI, humans, avatars, magical particles, external objects or logos. No black or white background.
```

The original generated image remains separate from the final game sprite.
